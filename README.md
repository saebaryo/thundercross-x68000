# Thunder Cross for X68000

An unofficial fan port of Konami's 1988 arcade shooter *Thunder Cross* to the
Sharp X68000. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Konami.

**→ https://saebaryo.github.io/thundercross-x68000/**

This repository holds the distribution page and nothing else.

## What the page does

Add the 19 ROM files you already have (from MAME's `thunderx` and `thunderxj`
sets — the two program ROMs must be from the Japanese version). The page
converts them in your browser and gives you `ThunderCrossX68k.xdf`, a bootable
floppy disk image for the X68000.

Your ROMs are never uploaded — the conversion runs entirely on your own
machine. You can even save the page and open it offline; it works the same.

**No game data is published here.** Neither this repository nor the page
contains any data from the original game. The graphics, sprites and sound on
your disk image are converted from the ROM files you supply.

The target machine is an X68000 XVI (16 MHz) with 4 MB of memory. The port has
been tested on a real X68000 XVI, XEiJ (X68000 and X68030 modes), XM6 TypeG and
the MiSTer FPGA X68000 core. Not tested on a real X68030.
